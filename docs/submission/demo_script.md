# 2-3 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:20 - Hook

"Every Indian gets a bank statement, but almost nobody can read one. PULSE treats that statement like a financial X-ray. The app is the radiologist."

Show the landing page, privacy promise, and supported bank logos.

## 0:20-0:45 - Upload

Click `Use sample statement`.

"This is Priya Sharma, a 28-year-old software engineer in Bangalore. She earns Rs 85,000/month, has a SIP, pays rent on time, but has stress spending and subscription leaks."

Let the scan animation run.

## 0:45-1:30 - Body Scan

Show the grade and body. Hover/click:

- Heart: savings rate
- Lungs: liquidity
- Stomach: salary-week spending discipline
- Spine: income stability
- Brain: investment ratio

"The body makes money understandable. You don't need to read 60 rows; you can see which organ needs attention."

## 1:30-1:55 - Leak Map

Open Leaks.

"These are the quiet drips: subscriptions under Rs 500 that repeat monthly. They look small alone, but together they reduce savings every month."

## 1:55-2:20 - Stress Signature

Open Stress Map.

"This is the key India-specific insight: day-of-month spending, not day-of-week. PULSE detects post-salary relief spending."

## 2:20-2:45 - Opportunity Engine

Open Opportunity.

"This is the emotional closer. If Priya redirects a small part of food delivery or streaming into her SIP, PULSE shows the 20-year wealth impact instantly."

## 2:45-3:00 - Close

"PULSE is private, stateless, and built for financial inclusion. Your money has a heartbeat. Now you can read it."
